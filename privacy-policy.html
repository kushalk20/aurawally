This app allows users to select photos from their device library or folders and set them as wallpaper.

Information We Access

To provide the wallpaper feature, the app may access photos selected by the user from the device gallery or local folders. This access is only used to display and apply the selected image as the device wallpaper.

What We Do Not Do

We do not upload, sell, share, or store user photos on our servers. We do not collect or share personal data such as health data, location data, contacts, or device identifiers for advertising or analytics purposes.

Local Storage

The app may store local preferences on the device to remember wallpaper settings and user-selected options. This data remains on the device and is not transmitted to us.

Third Parties

This app does not use third-party advertising or analytics services that collect personal data. We do not share user photos or personal information with third parties.
